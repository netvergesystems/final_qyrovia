How We Work

Four-Phase
Delivery Process

Our structured methodology ensures consistent quality, minimal disruption, and full client visibility from kickoff to handover.

01
Survey
Site audit and requirement gathering. We assess cable paths, rack placement, and power availability before any work begins.
02
Design
Planning cable routes, rack layout, heat dissipation, and port mapping. All documented before execution starts.
03
Execution
Professional installation with minimal operational disruption. Every cable labeled and organized from day one.
04
Handover
Full As-Built diagrams, cable test reports, and a signed Handover Document delivered to the client.
